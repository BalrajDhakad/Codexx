Process

  1. 01

    Understand

    What must the viewer feel, and in how many seconds.

  2. 02

    Explore

    Fast boards and rough timing. Kill weak ideas early.

  3. 03

    Design

    Frames, type and colour locked before anything moves.

  4. 04

    Animate

    Easing, weight and rhythm, one beat at a time.

  5. 05

    Refine

    Trim, retime, export. Ship it clean.
